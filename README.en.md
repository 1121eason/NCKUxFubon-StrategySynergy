# Fubon Credit Cards: Strategy and Synergy Evaluation

### Connecting competitive diagnosis, managerial attention and value realization

[繁體中文](README.md) · [Case analysis](docs/case-study.zh-TW.md) · [Theory to practice](docs/theory-to-practice.md) · [Execution design](docs/strategy-execution.md) · [Sources](docs/sources.md)

**How can customer acquisition, digital investment and acquisitions create lasting value?**

This historical case study examines Taipei Fubon Bank's credit card business. It combines a 2025 National Cheng Kung University Business Policy and Strategy group report, which Eason jointly contributed to in its entirety, with his individual presentation on synergy evaluation. The public edition develops the analysis through evidence corrections, explicit strategic choices and proposed measurement tools.

![From strategic diagnosis to value realization](assets/strategy-map.svg)

## Strategic argument

Scale expands the opportunity set. Realizing value requires reliable services, customer economics and coordinated execution. Four connected choices structure the analysis:

| Question | Strategic implication | Proposed action |
|---|---|---|
| Does digital investment improve adoption? | Evaluate customer task completion and continued use | Prioritize essential card journeys and measure service outcomes |
| Can ESG create meaningful engagement? | Separate participation, estimates and verified environmental outcomes | Validate customer needs and calculation methods before making impact claims |
| Does growth demonstrate acquisition synergy? | Separate the Jih Sun acquisition from the Costco partnership and from market growth | Track functional, business model and strategic initiatives independently |
| What does international expansion mean? | Outbound spending by Taiwan cardholders differs from local issuing abroad | Define the market and required capabilities before choosing an entry approach |

## Individual research focus

Eason's individual presentation interprets Bauer and Friesl's attention-based account of synergy evaluation. The study explains how M&A attention structures and valuation practices can favor readily quantified functional synergies, crowding out consideration of business model and strategic opportunities. Its 50 interviews were conducted by the researchers, not by Eason. The case does not establish that the same mechanism occurred inside Fubon. [Original research](https://doi.org/10.1111/joms.12804)

The practical extension is a governance design: use different evidence for different synergy types; reserve cross-functional review time; assign baselines, owners, costs and stopping conditions. The three categories are not a universal ranking of risk or returns, and business model synergies can also involve cost changes.

## Evidence that changes the interpretation

Coursework figures imply approximately 50.54% growth in cards in force and 67.59% in active cards during 2023. The bank's consolidated annual report shows operating expenses rising about 26.51% and net profit falling about 2.06%. Card metrics and bank-wide financial measures have different scopes. None isolates the effect of the acquisition. [Official 2023 annual report, printed pp. 196 and 209](https://www.fubon.com/banking/document/about/intro_FBB/TW/TaipeiFubonBank112.pdf)

The merger chronology is corrected: Fubon and Jih Sun financial holdings merged on 11 November 2022; the banks merged on 1 April 2023. The original coursework's expected 2025 completion date is not retained. [Official merger announcement](https://www.fubon.com/financialholdings/news/news_1230411_209213.htm)
